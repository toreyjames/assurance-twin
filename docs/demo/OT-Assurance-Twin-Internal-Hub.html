Value & Benefits

Deloitte helps clients move from incomplete OT asset lists to defensible assurance evidence. The Twin reconciles as-designed, as-built, and as-operated sources into asset-level claims that can support security, topology, risk, and client requirement discussions.

Defensible denominator

Documented, discovered, blind, and orphaned assets in one evidence model.

Evidence per claim

Each asset carries match confidence, source basis, and epistemic status.

Reusable client lenses

Industry profiles translate the same engine into DOT, utilities, oil and gas, and more.

Who is this for internally?

Partners, account teams, CPS/OT practitioners, and AI/data teams who need a crisp way to explain why asset visibility is an evidence and engineering problem, not only a tooling problem.

Framework / Solution

The Twin makes explicit how we know what we know about OT assets: where sources agree, where engineering expects a device discovery never saw, and where the network shows endpoints engineering did not document.

As-designedEngineering baseline, expected devices, criticality intent, locations.
As-builtDiscovery endpoints, identity (IP/MAC/hostname), observed protocols.
As-operatedMaintenance windows, lifecycle, telemetry freshness, vulnerability context.
As-assuredOne reconciled asset claim with evidence objects, confidence, and provenance.

Solution Capabilities

Reconciled inventoryMatched, blind (documented not seen), orphaned (seen not documented), inferred classification, unknown.
Evidence trailClaim basis, match logic, source excerpts, checksums and pipeline version where configured.
Risk translationTopology and segmentation lens, CVE exposure posture, RFI or control-family crosswalk without replacing SME judgment.
